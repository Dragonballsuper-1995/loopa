Frequently Asked Questions

Is Loopa completely free?

Yes! Loopa is 100% free to use. There are no paid tiers, hidden subscriptions, or annoying ads.

Does Loopa work without internet?

Yes. Your watchlist is always stored locally on your device. Any changes you make while offline are queued and automatically uploaded once you're back online.

How do AI recommendations work?

When you visit For You, our AI looks at what titles and genres you love in your watchlist and discovers hidden gems tailored to your taste.

Can I export or backup my watchlist?

Yes! In the profile menu (or Settings in the App), open Data Portability to download your full list in JSON or CSV format, or import a list anytime.

Is there an Android app?

Yes! Loopa is built natively for Android with offline support, fluid Jetpack Compose UI, and instant cloud sync.

Quick Troubleshooting

Sync Not Updating?

Open the profile menu and click Sync Data. Ensure you are signed in with the same email on both Web and Android.

Missing Posters or Data?

Loopa retrieves metadata from TMDB and AniList. If a title is brand new or rare, check back soon or try searching with the alternative English/Japanese title.
