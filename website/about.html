About Loopa

Built for People Who Love Stories

Loopa was made to make tracking everything you watch effortless, beautiful, and completely your own.

Our Mission

Most streaming apps are cluttered, slow, and try to sell you subscriptions you don’t need. Loopa is different. It is a clean, lightning-fast media tracker designed to be your single command center for movies, TV series, and anime.

Lightning Fast 100% Ad-Free Instant Cloud Sync

Core Features

Radar Search

Find movies, series, and anime instantly across global databases with rich summaries, cast info, and high-res posters.

My List & Progress

Organize your media into Watching, Plan to Watch, and Watched. Log episode counts and personal ratings with a single tap.

AI "For You"

Discover your next obsession with personalized AI recommendations that adapt to your taste without annoying algorithms.
